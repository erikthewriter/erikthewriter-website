**No Survivors**

*Book One of the Day of the End Series*

Humanity didn’t collapse into chaos.  
It evolved past it.

War ended. Hunger vanished. Nations united under progress, proof, and reason. Everything that couldn’t be measured, tested, or verified was quietly left behind.

For decades, the world was peaceful.

Then, in less than an hour, it ended.

When impossible creatures erupt into the cities, tearing through populations with terrifying precision, communication collapses and governments vanish overnight. Entire regions go dark. There are no warnings. No explanations. No rules anyone understands.

James Ward Norter survives the first night—but survival doesn’t make sense anymore. The creatures behave differently around him. They hesitate. They follow. And as the body count rises, so does the quiet fear among the living that Ward may not be entirely on their side of whatever is happening.

As Ward joins a small, fractured group of survivors, they’re forced onto the roads between ruined cities, searching for safety in a world that no longer recognizes humanity as anything special. Trust erodes. Loss compounds. And something unseen watches their every step.

No Survivors is a dark, atmospheric apocalypse told through tension, dread, and unraveling reality—where the monsters are real, the answers are not, and staying alive may come at a cost worse than death.

This is not a story about saving the world.  
It’s about what’s left when the world no longer wants you.
