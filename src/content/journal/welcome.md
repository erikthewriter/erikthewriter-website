If there's one thing I've learned over the years, it's that nothing ever stays quite the same. Things grow, priorities shift, and sometimes what once made perfect sense needs to be torn down and rebuilt.

For a long time, **Erik Brown, the author**, and **EriktheWriter, the gamer and streamer**, existed in two separate corners of the internet. One was dedicated to storytelling, novels, and the written word. The other was filled with gaming, late-night streams, ridiculous adventures, and an incredible community of people I've come to know as *The Library*.

But the truth is, they've always been the same person.

So, after plenty of changes, more than a few headaches, and a fair amount of rebuilding, I'm finally bringing those two worlds together under one roof.

This new website will be home to everything I create and everything I'm passionate about. Books, writing updates, gaming, livestreams, community projects, and whatever strange ideas I decide to chase next.

There are still plenty of changes ahead, and I fully expect to break a few things along the way. But that's part of building something worthwhile.

Whether you found me through the pages of a book or the chaos of a Twitch stream, I'm glad you're here.

**Welcome to the new home of Erik Brown and EriktheWriter. The stories continue, the games are loading, and The Library is officially open.**
